Medical Capabilities

24 Hours Medical Services with Modern Equipment

Full spectrum of neonatal, pediatric, and critical care units engineered for quick recovery.

👶

Neonatal Intensive Care (NICU)

  • Preterm infant care with advanced artificial ventilator respiratory support
  • Dual surface phototherapy units for neonatal jaundice management
  • Exchange blood transfusion facility for severe hyperbilirubinemia
  • Central pipeline oxygen, multipara monitors, and precision pulse oximeters
  • 24/7 dedicated neonatologist and trained neonatal nursing team
❤️

Pediatric Intensive Care (PICU)

  • Specialized critical care for pediatric congenital & acquired heart ailments
  • Treatment of severe pneumonia, respiratory distress, and childhood asthma
  • Management of convulsions, seizures, epilepsy, and brain infections
  • Treatment for toxic fevers, dengue shock syndrome, and poisoning care
  • Continuous 24-hour pediatric intensivist monitoring
🔬

24/7 Lab, Mobile X-Ray & Pharmacy

  • Mobile digital X-Ray facility for instant bed-side radiography
  • Automatic biochemistry and hematology analyzers for round-the-clock tests
  • Immediate emergency blood, urine, electrolyte, and infection reports
  • 24-hour fully stocked in-house pharmacy
  • Specialized pediatric medicines, syrups, and emergency infusions
🏛️

Government Schemes & Surgery

  • Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva cashless treatment for eligible families
  • Employees Health Scheme (E.H.S) coverage for government employees
  • 0 to 18 years complete pediatric healthcare services
  • State-of-the-art sterile modular operation theatre
  • 24-hour dedicated emergency ambulance service
  • All newborn and childhood vaccinations (Immunization Clinic)
